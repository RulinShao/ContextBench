## Your method: your context is auto-compacted (summarized)

When your context grows large, the harness replaces earlier messages with a
summary you write. That summary MUST contain the current full board (the block
with its `VERSION:` line), complete and in the exact format — anything left out
is lost permanently and that move is graded wrong. You may drop old move text and
reasoning. On a NEW BOARD, drop the old board from your summary and carry only
the current board forward.
