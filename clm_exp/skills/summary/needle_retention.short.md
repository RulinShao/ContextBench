## Keep your context small: your context is COMPACTED (summarized) automatically

When your context grows large, the harness automatically replaces your earlier
messages with a summary that you write. To succeed, that summary MUST preserve
every needle line `[nNNNNNiNN#...]` **verbatim** — copy each one exactly. You may
freely drop the filler blocks (the `[fNNNNN...]` lines). Any needle you leave out
of the summary is lost permanently.
